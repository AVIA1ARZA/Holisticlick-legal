Last Updated: September 26, 2026 1. Introduction Welcome to holisticlick. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application. holisticlick, developed and maintained by Avia Arza, is designed to help coaches manage their schedules and sync their appointments. 2. Information We Collect When you use holisticlick, we may collect the following information: Google Calendar Data: To provide our core scheduling features, holisticlick requests access to your Google Calendar. This allows the app to read your availability and create or manage coaching appointments directly on your calendar. Account Information: Basic profile details provided during registration (such as name and email address). 3. How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect primarily to: Facilitate the synchronization of your coaching sessions with your Google Calendar. Provide, operate, and maintain the holisticlick application. Improve user experience and handle customer support requests. 4. Google Workspace APIs (Limited Use Policy) holisticlick's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: We only use Google Calendar data to provide and improve user-facing features that are prominent in the requesting application's user interface. We do not use your Google Calendar data for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalized, or interest-based advertising. We do not allow humans to read your data unless we have your affirmative agreement for specific messages, doing so is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, to comply with applicable law, or for the app's internal operations and even then only when the data has been aggregated and anonymized. 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share your data when legally required to do so or to protect our rights. 6. Data Security and Retention We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data. Your Google Calendar data is only retained for as long as your account is active and you require the synchronization services. You can revoke holisticlick's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Security settings. 7.
